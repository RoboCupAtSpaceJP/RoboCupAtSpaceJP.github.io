01 / OVERVIEW

Overview

@Space Challenge is a competition designed to advance AI × Space × Robotics technologies for future space activities in orbit, on the Moon, and on other planetary bodies.

@Space Challenge was established as a new category of RoboCup JapanOpen, a competition for autonomous robots. The first edition is based on a scenario using Int-Ball2, an ISS internal robot developed by JAXA.

Using the Int-Ball2 Gazebo simulator, teams compete on tasks such as autonomous navigation, manipulation, and cooperative operations, broadening participation in space robotics development through both education and research. From the second edition onward, the competition aims to expand toward the use of the actual Int-Ball2 aboard the ISS and establish a benchmark for autonomous space robots.

02 / COMPETITION

Competition

The first edition includes the main competition, a technical exchange session, the Open Category (competition proposals and demonstrations), and an Exhibition.

MAIN TASK

Autonomous Inspection and Verification Tasks inside a Space Station

  1. 01
    Autonomous Navigation to the Target

    Navigate autonomously from the docking station to the target while avoiding astronauts.

  2. 02
    Multi-View Imaging

    Detect the target object and capture images from multiple viewpoints while keeping the object centered in the frame.

  3. 03
    Autonomous Return to the Station

    Return while avoiding astronauts and dock autonomously with the docking station.

Teams share their technical approaches and research through posters and presentations, promoting information exchange and discussion among participants.

Exhibition

Interested teams give free-form presentations or demonstrations to showcase research outcomes and technologies under development.

OPEN CATEGORY

Competition Proposals & Demonstrations

Each team presents original ideas or research outcomes in a 10-minute presentation followed by a 5-minute Q&A session. RoboCup representatives evaluate the presentations, and outstanding entries are recognized.

  1. OriginalityNovelty and uniqueness of the idea
  2. Feasibility in SpaceCompatibility with environmental constraints and relevance to RoboCup
  3. Social / Mission ImpactValue for space development and society
  4. Demonstration Success & PersuasivenessVerifiability and reproducibility
  5. PresentationCommunication quality and Q&A
