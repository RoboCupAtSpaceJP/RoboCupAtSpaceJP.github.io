RoboCup JapanOpen @Space Challenge is a robotics competition designed to advance AI × Space × Robotics technologies for future activities in orbit, on the Moon, and on other planetary bodies.

01 / ABOUT

About
@Space Challenge

Participants develop algorithms and integrate systems for autonomous space robots, competing on problem-solving tasks inspired by real operations. The challenge also aims to build a community connecting education, research, and industry.

01
◎

Objective

Education and research on advanced autonomous space robotics technologies, including AI, control, perception, and navigation.

02
◇

Participants

Open to a broad range of participants involved in space robotics, including students, professionals, research institutions, and companies.

03
⌁

Competition Format

Tasks and evaluation metrics are designed around scenarios in which robots operate in actual space environments.

03 / NEWS

News

Latest updates on activities, awards, and tournaments

  1. 2026.09.04

    Two presentations, including a keynote lecture, were delivered at the 44th Annual Conference of the Robotics Society of Japan (RSJ2026).

  2. 2026.04.27

    The @Space Challenge received the Robotics Society of Japan Award for its competition platform promoting the application of ground-based AI technologies to space.

  3. 2026.04.25–27

    RoboCup JapanOpen 2026 @Space Challenge was held as an autonomous space robotics competition in an ISS-simulated environment.

  4. 2026.03.06

    The RoboCup JapanOpen @Space Challenge tournament website was launched.

  5. 2026.02.28

    An invited lecture was delivered at the RoboCup Workshop, a 30th-anniversary special project of the Kayamori Foundation of Informational Science Advancement.

  6. 2025.12.13–14

    A RoboCup @Space Challenge tutorial was held at the 13th Intelligent Home Robotics Workshop.

  7. 2025.09.03

    Two presentations on @Space Challenge were delivered at the 43rd Annual Conference of the Robotics Society of Japan (RSJ2025).
